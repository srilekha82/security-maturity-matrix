How the two frameworks relate

NIST CSF 2.0

The Cybersecurity Framework covers how an organisation protects its systems, data, and operations. It addresses the security of the environment in which AI runs — but not the AI itself.

  • Asset inventory & access controls
  • Incident detection & response
  • Data protection & resilience
  • Third-party security

NIST AI RMF 1.0

The AI Risk Management Framework covers the trustworthiness, accountability, and governance of AI tools and systems themselves — risks that exist even when the underlying infrastructure is secure.

  • Trustworthy AI principles & policies
  • AI use-case risk classification
  • Third-party AI vendor assessment
  • Bias, output quality & auditability

Together, these two assessments give {{ORG_NAME}} a complete view of its risk posture across both conventional cybersecurity and the emerging AI risk surface.
